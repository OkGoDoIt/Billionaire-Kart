# Davos Circuit

A 1,490 m alpine circuit climbs from the private-jet apron to a 70 m summit. The same town that disappears beneath the switchbacks comes back into view when the kart clears the ski jump. Three laps take the valley from golden hour to pink alpenglow.

## The racing line

| Control points | Section | Driving decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 | Jet Corner, right | The flag-lined resort straight turns uphill beside the aircraft. Collect the first item row on the approach. |
| 2–5 | The Promenade | Follow the coins across the cobbles or stay clear of the snowplow on the edge of the village. |
| 5–9 | Switchback One, left | Carry the drift around the 22 m hairpin. The exit boost sits off center on the inside. |
| 10–14 | Switchback Two, right | Reverse the drift and aim for the opposite edge pad. The ridge beyond opens the sight line to the summit. |
| 15–19 | Cowbell slalom | Three alternating red and blue gates mark a coin line across the traverse. The last gate pays a narrow boost. The middle stays clear for players taking the direct line. |
| 19–21 | Summit Bend, right | Tighten the line at 70 m, then choose a takeoff lane before the in-run drops away. |
| 21–24 | The Great Reset | The 13 m wide table offers a forgiving launch. A 3.6 m kicker on the left climbs 6.4 m and sends a well-aimed kart through the high coins. Trick on takeoff, look for the painted distance lines, and set up for the turn below. |
| 24–26 | Outrun, left | Clear the judged landing and carry speed into the stadium bend. The three snow-delegate scores reward a different kind of performance. |
| 26–30 | Security Sweep, right | Take the west descent past the helipad, hold the outside fencing in view, and drift back through the checkpoint. |
| 30–0 | The resort | Finish the drift onto the level grid straight and start the climb again. |

## Staging

The opening camera looks across the valley at the summit and landing hill. The second flies out over the lip. The hand-shaped snow shelf fills the depression where the landing hill meets the east piste. Two fluted peaks and snow-covered ledges break up the massif behind the tower. The normal road stays dark against the snow, with cobbles on the Promenade and a snow surface on the competition hill.

Promenade placards and hillside sponsors face a kart roughly one second before it reaches them. Trees are excluded from the line between sign and approach. Alternating flags, irregular snow-laden firs, a swinging brass bell, and the summit tower distinguish the traverse from the switchbacks. The timing stripes on the landing slope are flush with the road and drawn to follow its grade. Animated skiers and a gondola occupy the slopes beside the jump. The stadium, delegates, and congress hall frame the descent without interrupting the flight.

The layout and placements live in `src/tracks/davos.js`. The bell, flags, chalets, firs, and ski-jump structures live in `src/models/props-davos.js`. The painted horizon and its source prompts are under `assets/backdrops/`.
