# Real Company Logos — Sources

All files sourced from **Wikimedia Commons** (PNG renders of the official logo files
hosted there), downloaded 2026-11-10, trimmed of excess transparent margin and
normalized to 512px on the long edge. Every file was visually verified against the
real company identity before inclusion.

**License / trademark note (applies to all):** the Commons files are licensed as
shown per entry (nearly all public domain as simple text/geometric marks below the
threshold of copyright originality). Regardless of copyright status, **every logo
remains a registered trademark of its owner**. Use in Billionaire Kart is nominative
— identifying parody caricatures of public figures by their real companies — and
implies no affiliation or endorsement.
