Controls

Keyboard — WASD / arrows · Space = item · Shift = drift · R = reset · P = pause

Gamepad — stick steer · RT/A gas · LT brake · X item · LB drift

Touch — on-screen pedals, or pick a Control Lab scheme above. Thumbstick: hold the right side = full gas, drag sideways to steer, pull down = full brake (the stick turns red + buzzes so you can't brake by accident), flick up = item, tap the left side = item. Dual Stick: left stick steers (quick tap = item), right stick drives — hold = gas, quick tap = drift hop (then hold the left stick turned to keep drifting), pull down = brake, flick up = item. In Dual Stick a split-second thumb lift won't cut your gas or steering — they latch for a beat (the stick stays lit gold). The item slot is always tappable in every scheme.

Mouse — hold click = gas, move mouse to steer

Items favour the losers. That's the point.

Leaders only draw defence — traps, Subpoenas, the Spin Team. Last place gets the full comeback kit. The one counter that matters: ⚖️ Antitrust Suit hunts 1st place, and a well-timed 📣 Spin Team swats it out of the air. Full plain-language rundown of all 13 items in section 6 above.

The skill move

Hold drift through corners until the sparks turn blue, then orange — release for a boost. Leaders get data leaks. Last place gets the fun stuff. Stay scrappy.
