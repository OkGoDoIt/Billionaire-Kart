# International Waters driving review

The three lounge views in `salons.jpg` are the actual race camera at control points 23.9, 29.5, and 35.8 on the main-branch yacht layout. The lounge mural, teak-to-carpet transition, and ceiling lights render in all three.

The baseline medium phone performance probe at 0%, 20%, 40%, 60%, and 80% of the lap returned 533, 578, 213, 337, and 177 draw calls. The peak triangle count was 718,000.

The same channel camera, 2.4 seconds apart, appears in `swell.jpg`. The second yacht moves from +0.819 m to −0.819 m relative to its nominal deck height. The first moves from 0 to +0.247 m and the third from +0.141 m to −0.471 m. A separate 150cc full-throttle single-kart sweep drove every gap at 32 arrival times, at both high and low tide. All 256 crossings went airborne and landed without a rescue. Six normal field races averaged 0.2 rescues and 27.8 hits. Six hard field races averaged 0.7 rescues and 31.7 hits.
